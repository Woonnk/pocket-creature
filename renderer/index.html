Talk to your dragon

Commands you can say

“Take a nap”, “go home”, “check your toys”, “wake up”, “give him a snack”, “play fetch”, “start a 25-minute focus session”, “pause focus”, “resume focus”, “end focus”, “repeat that”, “stop talking”, or “be quiet”, or “remind me in 10 minutes to drink water”. These run on this device without Ollama. Other messages continue your conversation. You can type commands too.

How push-to-talk works

Offline Windows dictation. Release to send; each recording is limited to 20 seconds and held in memory only. Conversation text enters your normal chat history; creature commands do not. Enable Spoken replies to hear his answer. Quiet mode pauses the shortcut. Turn Quiet off using the Companion toggle to talk again.

Your data & controls

Reactions use the current window title, app category, idle time, and typing counts. Window titles are transient and aren’t saved; a reply may mention what it observes. Supported editor titles spark filename reactions automatically. File contents are inspected inside folders you connect once. You can also choose a file explicitly.
