# Pocket Creature 0.23.0 — favorites, friendship, and a more expressive dragon

This beta brings the accumulated 0.16–0.23 updates to GitHub, with a refreshed project page and Windows download.

- **Four dragon looks:** Original, Cloud Drake, Night Wyvern and River Dragon. Free switching keeps your colors, personality, expressions and progress.
- **Smoother movement:** independent animated joints, moving paws, breathing, wings, tail, blinks, soft landings and eased transitions between poses.
- **Fresher reactions:** app/file changes settle before comments, typing and conversations take priority, and superseded observations are discarded.
- **Voice and body language:** Kokoro mouth movement follows playing WAV samples; supported Windows voices use mouth-position or word events. Phrase gestures and personality posture add expression. Optional local chirps have their own volume and start off.
- **Learned favorites:** repeated snacks, care and completed manual play teach preferences after three visits at least a minute apart. Review, pin, pause or forget them under Creature → Our bond. Favorite games share editable Personal memory and come from your statements or edits.
- **Friendship milestones:** unlock Tiny nuzzle, Paw promise, Wing hug and Happy flourish at 10/25/50/100 friendship. Previous friendship counts, old milestones unlock quietly, and new notices leave the current conversation intact.
- **Quiet company and helpers:** facial expressions, paw offers, real toy visits, trick school, small screen adventures, an attention badge, reminders, opt-in Windows startup, backup export/import and clearer local connection checks.

**Windows:** download `Pocket-Creature-0.23.0-Windows.exe`. Quit the previous copy from its tray before launching. Existing progress and settings remain compatible on save schema 15. Keep the portable app in a permanent folder before enabling startup. The executable is unsigned.

**Optional services:** Ollama/models and Kokoro are installed separately. Windows voices need no Kokoro service. The companion requires no account or API key. Microphone input is opt-in; screen snapshots default off.

**Browser:** `Pocket-Creature-Playground.html` previews the creature with separate browser progress. Desktop sensing, native microphone recognition and local services require Windows.

The local verification record includes 153 logic tests, 23 browser suites, exact runtime-source checks and extraction of the finished portable. GitHub's release workflow independently tests, compiles the Windows helpers and packages checksummed assets before publishing. Direct native Windows microphone/audio, DPI/monitor behavior, live Ollama/Kokoro and exclusive-fullscreen acceptance remain documented in [QA.md](https://github.com/Woonnk/pocket-creature/blob/main/QA.md).

This is a **pre-release beta**, before stable 1.0.
